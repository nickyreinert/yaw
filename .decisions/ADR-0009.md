# ADR-0009: Terminology and preset contents (Quick / Standard / Full)

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Category:** Modules / Catalogue
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Importance:** normal

## Context

"Preset" meant three things, Lean and Quick were the same, and the phase-1 contents of Standard and Full, the footprint and the AGENTS.md size were open (BD5).

## Decision

- Say "mode" (quick / standard / full) and "saved setup"; "preset" only in `yaw presets` and the manifest field.
- Quick = core, communication, standards, devenv, tasks + minimal security; Standard adds git-hooks, github, ci, decisions, sessions, comfort; Full adds the F questions and team options.
- Quick asks only the Q questions of its modules; AGENTS.md has a hard limit of 80 lines (render error).
- Without `decisions` the proposal stays in answers.yaml; `yaw decisions seed` records it once the module is added.

## Consequences

Docs 02 and 04 follow these words and numbers; a module that needs more AGENTS.md lines moves detail to docs/spec or a skill.
