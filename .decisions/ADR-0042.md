# ADR-0042: Interview expertise: three levels (layperson, intermediate, expert); doc 02 section 4 gets a third column

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Category:** Architecture / CLI
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Importance:** normal

## Context

Interview 0.1 and the answers schema (user.expertise, the communication style, the user-profile schema) have three levels; doc 02 section 4 defines two (expert, layperson) (open issue P7). The full CLI interview (Y-7.1) has to act on the level.

## Decision

- Keep three levels. Dropping one would change the answer schema, the user-profile schema, existing answers files and the communication module for no gain; intermediate is the default.
- Doc 02 section 4 gets a third column. In the CLI: layperson sees the help text of a question before it is asked and F questions take their default (listed as default in the summary); intermediate gets help on request (?) and every question of the mode; expert is like intermediate with hints collapsed behind ?.
- The level applies from the question after user.expertise (0.1) on; before that, and when unanswered, intermediate. Wording variants and the form's collapsible blocks are Y-7.4.

## Consequences

No new answer key and no schema change. The F questions are the questions with safe defaults that a layperson does not get (doc 02 section 4 row Questions). Doc 02 section 4 and the plan row P7 follow in the same PR.
